How does the AI Resume Review work?

Upload your resume and our AI analyzes structure, content, formatting, and ATS compatibility. You receive a detailed report with actionable recommendations in under a minute.

What file formats do you support?

We support PDF and DOCX formats — the two most common resume formats accepted by employers and ATS systems.

Will this help me pass ATS systems?

Yes. We identify ATS compatibility issues and suggest precise improvements to increase the likelihood your resume reaches a human recruiter.

Is my resume data secure?

Absolutely. Your resume is processed securely and remains confidential. We never share or sell your data to third parties.

Can I upload multiple versions?

Yes. Upload updated versions as you make improvements and track your score progress over time.

Is it suitable for students and fresh graduates?

Absolutely. The platform works for all experience levels — from first-time applicants to senior executives.

Does the platform rewrite my resume?

It provides specific, actionable recommendations while keeping you in full control of your final edits and personal voice.
